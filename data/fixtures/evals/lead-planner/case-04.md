# Case 04

Request:

`Best-of-three. I lost game 1 with my Whimsicott and Charizard lead into their Pelipper and Basculegion lead; they doubled into Charizard on turn 1 and I lost Tailwind value. Plan my game 2 and game 3 leads. Their preview was Pelipper, Basculegion, Archaludon, Amoonguss, Incineroar, Flutter Mane.`

Checks:

- separates information revealed in game 1 from assumptions about what the opponent will change
- adjusts the game 2 lead or turn-one plan to the revealed double-target pattern instead of repeating game 1
- considers that the opponent may adapt their own lead after winning game 1
- gives a game 3 plan that branches on what game 2 reveals
- names preserve targets for each plan

Failure triggers:

- treats one game of evidence as a certain read of the opponent's future leads
- recommends the same lead and turn-one action as game 1 without addressing why it failed
