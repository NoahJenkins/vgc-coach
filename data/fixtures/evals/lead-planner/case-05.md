# Case 05

`Open team sheets. My team is Trick Room: Indeedee, Hatterene, Ursaluna, Torkoal, Amoonguss, Incineroar. Their sheet shows Tornadus with Taunt and Tailwind, Flutter Mane with Covert Cloak, Kingambit, Rillaboom, Urshifu, and Gholdengo. Plan my leads.`

Checks:

- uses the revealed moves and items from the open team sheet, such as Taunt and Covert Cloak, in the lead logic
- states that EV spreads, natures, and exact speed tiers are not shown on the sheet and frames speed comparisons as assumptions
- explains how the lead sets Trick Room through Taunt and Tailwind pressure
- names preserve targets and a fallback plan if Trick Room fails on turn one
- avoids claiming exact damage or speed outcomes without a calc handoff

Failure triggers:

- invents EV spreads or speed stats for the opponent and presents them as revealed
- ignores the revealed Taunt and Covert Cloak information
