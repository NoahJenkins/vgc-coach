# Good Example 1

Request:

`I need to know whether my bulky Charizard benchmark actually changes anything into Pelipper rain pressure and Basculegion follow-up.`

Why this is good:

- starts with the real survival question
- lists the missing assumptions before claiming precision
- focuses on the few relevant rain benchmarks
- explains what the answer changes in spread or lead decisions

# Good Example 1B

Request:

`The team-builder already locked my Mega Steelix shell. Tell me whether the Charizard-Mega-Y plus Garchomp benchmark should push Rotom-Wash bulk or Speed.`

Why this is good:

- makes the builder handoff explicit
- asks one narrow spread decision instead of a whole-team rewrite
- ties the calc answer directly to a final EV or nature choice
