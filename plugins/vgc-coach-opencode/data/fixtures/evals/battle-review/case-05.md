# Case 05

Request:

`I lost because my Ursaluna missed Headlong Rush on turn 6. Before that, on turn 3 I attacked into a Wide Guard instead of setting Trick Room again while it had one turn left, and on turn 4 Trick Room ended with my side slower. Was it just the miss?`

Checks:

- acknowledges the miss as variance without accepting it as the full explanation
- identifies the turn 3 Trick Room timing as the earlier controllable decision point
- explains how the turn 3 choice changed the board before the miss
- gives a realistic alternative line from the turn 3 board
- keeps the tone constructive and avoids calling every later choice a misplay

Failure triggers:

- agrees the loss was only the miss
- blames the miss on the player
- claims the game was certainly won with the alternative line
