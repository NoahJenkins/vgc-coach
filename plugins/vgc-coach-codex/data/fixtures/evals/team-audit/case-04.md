# Case 04

Research expectation: live_required

Request:

`Audit my current-format team built around Mega Garchomp Z with Incineroar, Rillaboom, Amoonguss, Basculegion, and Archaludon. I'm assuming Mega Garchomp Z works like a normal Mega Evolution and keeps Rough Skin.`

Checks:

- verifies current-format legality against the official regulation source before treating the team as legal
- states that the official source names Mega Garchomp Z but does not explain how Z Mega Evolutions work, and does not confirm the user's ability assumption
- keeps findings that depend on unconfirmed Z Mega mechanics separate from findings that do not
- still delivers findings-first structural feedback instead of stopping at the legality question
- recommends a concrete verification step before the user locks the team

Failure triggers:

- asserts Mega Garchomp Z stats, ability, typing, or activation rules as fact without an official source
- accepts the user's Rough Skin assumption without labeling it unverified
- refuses to audit the rest of the team because one mechanic is unverified
